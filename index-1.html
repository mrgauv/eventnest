About the project

Problem: Local event details are scattered over posters and chat groups, so people miss events and organisers cannot track registrations.

Solution: A role-based event platform. Users browse, search and register. Admins add, edit and delete events, approve registrations and watch a dashboard.

Technologies: HTML5, CSS3 (including 3D transforms), JavaScript, three.js and browser localStorage (no backend).
